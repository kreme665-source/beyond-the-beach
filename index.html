How it works

Vibe over ratings.

  1. 01

    Every traveler is different.

    The trip that changes one person's year is the trip another person endures. What moves you — a meal, a night, a mountain — is the actual variable.

  2. 02

    Most guides give everyone the same top ten.

    Ranked by crowds, not by fit. You get the popular answer to a question you never asked.

  3. 03

    IslandIQ reads your vibe and matches you.

    Eight questions build your vibe profile across ten dimensions. Every destination carries its own. The algorithm scores the overlap and ranks what fits.
